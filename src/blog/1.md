Chess is a board game for two players, played on a square board consisting of 64 squares arranged in an 8x8 grid.
The players, referred to as White and Black, each control 16 pieces:

* 1 King (K)
* 1 Queen (Q)
* 2 Rooks (R)
* 2 Knights (N)
* 2 Bishops (B), and
* 8 Pawns

An enemy piece may be captured by moving one's own piece onto the square it occupies.
The object of the game is to checkmate the enemy King.
